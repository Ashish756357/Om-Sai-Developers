# Om Sai Developers Website Design Crawl

## Design Direction

The website is a warm, editorial real-estate landing page for bungalow plots. Its visual language combines:

- Cream paper surfaces and muted sage backgrounds.
- Deep forest green for trust, navigation, and high-information sections.
- Warm orange and peach accents for calls to action, prices, highlights, and emphasis.
- Serif display typography for an aspirational, residential feeling.
- Sans-serif utility typography for labels, forms, details, and navigation.
- Thin borders, restrained shadows, generous spacing, and square or lightly rounded surfaces.
- Soft shader grain in the hero for a tactile printed-paper atmosphere.

The overall tone is calm, grounded, transparent, and investment-oriented rather than aggressively sales-focused.

## 1. Fixed Navigation

The page begins with a sticky top navigation bar over the hero.

Visual design:

- Dark translucent forest-green glass surface.
- Frosted blur and a subtle light edge highlight.
- Brand mark at left with an orange circular badge and animated liquid-metal treatment.
- Brand label: Om Sai Developers.
- Small project label beneath the brand name.

Desktop behavior:

- Horizontal navigation links for highlights, amenities, master plan, location, ROI calculator, and contact.
- A prominent Book site visit button.

Mobile behavior:

- Navigation links collapse into a menu icon.
- The menu expands inside the same dark translucent header.

Primary conversion action: open the site-visit enquiry modal.

## 2. Hero Section

The hero is the first conversion surface and occupies most of the first viewport.

Visual design:

- Full-width dark green foundation.
- Cream, warm orange, and sage ShaderGradient background.
- Low-motion organic distortion.
- Soft grain/noise overlay for a printed texture.
- Dark vertical shade over the gradient to preserve text contrast.
- White and pale peach typography.

Content hierarchy:

1. Location eyebrow: Village Gundhle, Boisar East.
2. Large serif headline: Build your own bungalow.
3. Supporting offer copy explaining the 2,000 sq. ft. minimum, Rs. 1,500 per sq. ft. starting price, 77-acre setting, Boisar Station access, and Mumbai connectivity.
4. Builder proof row containing acreage, plot size, and price.
5. Frosted brochure/details form.
6. Direct phone contact line with both supplied numbers.

The 3D house model has been removed. The hero now relies on the shader atmosphere, copy hierarchy, proof points, and form rather than a decorative model.

Primary actions:

- Submit plot-detail form.
- Open the site-visit modal after form submission.
- Use the navigation or header CTA to move deeper into the page.

## 3. Proof Bar

A horizontal trust strip follows the hero immediately.

It uses a pale stone background, thin dividers, large orange numeric values, and compact uppercase descriptions.

Current proof points:

- 77 acres of green countryside.
- 2,000+ sq. ft. bungalow plots.
- Rs. 1,500 starting price per sq. ft.
- Clear title with separate 7/12.

Its purpose is to let a visitor scan the strongest buying facts before reading the longer sections.

## 4. Why Choose Om Sai Section

This section introduces the builder and converts abstract promises into buyer reassurance.

Layout:

- Left column contains a section label, serif heading, and short explanation.
- Right side contains four concise proof items in a two-column grid.

Proof items:

- Clear title and separate 7/12 extract.
- 77 acres of green countryside.
- Village Gundhle connectivity to Boisar and Mumbai.
- Direct phone access to the sales team.

The cards use a warm light background with dark green headings and dark sage body copy for strong readability.

## 5. Legacy Highlights Section

The next section is still structured as a traditional benefits section.

Layout:

- Left side: section label, large serif statement, supporting paragraph, and master-plan link.
- Right side: four feature cards in a two-column grid.

Feature themes:

- Clear legal titles.
- Gated security.
- Infrastructure.
- Investment potential.

The cards use light surfaces, orange line icons, dark headings, and readable supporting text.

Note: the current heading and supporting paragraph still reference the previous Serene Palms and Dapoli positioning. They should be rewritten to match the Boisar East property before launch.

## 6. Amenities and Infrastructure

This is a full-width forest-green information band.

Visual design:

- Dark green background.
- Cream heading with orange emphasis.
- Four columns separated by top borders.
- Pale peach line icons and muted light-green supporting text.

Content themes:

- Recreation and wellness.
- Sustainability.
- Utilities.
- Services and support.

The section is designed for scanning and communicates that the land offering includes a planned community experience, not only a raw parcel.

## 7. Master Plan and Plot Availability

This section changes from marketing copy to an interactive inventory view.

Layout:

- Section heading and availability legend at the top.
- Left side: plotted grid with numbered plots.
- Right side: explanatory location card with a CTA.

Plot states:

- Available: green surface and clickable.
- Booked: yellow surface and disabled.
- Sold: muted red surface and disabled.

Clicking an available plot opens a modal with plot number, area, orientation, and an enquiry CTA. This is the most direct product-selection interaction on the page.

Note: plot sizes and orientations currently contain values from the earlier Dapoli version and should be replaced with the real Boisar inventory.

## 8. Configurations

Two side-by-side offer panels present the ways a buyer can proceed.

- Option A: raw NA plots with flexibility to design independently.
- Option B: turnkey villas with construction support.

The first panel uses a cream surface and dark text. The second uses the warm orange brand color with cream text. Both include a direct contact link.

Note: the current copy still uses the previous raw-plot and Konkan villa positioning and should be aligned with the bungalow plot offer.

## 9. Location and Connectivity

This section pairs a factual access list with a stylized map graphic.

Left side:

- Section label and connectivity headline.
- Short location description.
- Stacked travel-time rows with orange time labels and dark location text.

Right side:

- Pale green map-like field.
- Abstract road lines and circular site markers.
- Central orange Serene Palms marker.
- Village location label.

Note: this entire block still references Dapoli, Khed, SH-96, beaches, and Serene Palms. It needs to be replaced with Boisar Station, Village Gundhle, Mumbai travel time, and verified nearby landmarks.

## 10. Investment Calculator

The calculator is another full-width forest-green band.

Layout:

- Left column explains the purpose and limitations of the estimate.
- Right column contains sliders and result panels.

Interactive controls:

- Plot area slider.
- Annual appreciation slider.
- Nightly villa rate slider.

Outputs:

- Estimated five-year capital gain.
- Projected monthly gross yield.

The calculator is framed as an indicative planning tool, not a guaranteed returns statement.

Note: the default land rate and rental assumptions should be reviewed for the Boisar East product before publishing investment projections.

## 11. Contact Section

The final major content section is a site-visit conversion area.

Layout:

- Left column contains the emotional close, visit invitation, and address.
- Right column contains a warm pale form panel.

Form fields:

- Name.
- WhatsApp number.
- Email address.
- Purchase timeline.
- Preferred visit date.

The form currently submits locally and closes the modal or completes the local interaction without a backend integration.

The address and visit-support copy still reference Village Nargoli and Dapoli and should be updated to Village Gundhle, Boisar East.

## 12. Footer and Floating Contact

The footer closes the page with a deep green background and muted sage text.

It contains:

- Om Sai Developers identity.
- Short builder statement.
- Back to top link.
- Brochure/contact link.
- Privacy policy placeholder link.
- WhatsApp link.
- Compliance note.
- Copyright line.

A fixed green WhatsApp button remains visible at the bottom-right and opens a prefilled enquiry message.

The WhatsApp destination now uses the supplied Om Sai Developers contact number, but footer copy and compliance language should be checked against the final project details.

## Responsive Design Behavior

On mobile widths:

- Navigation becomes a menu.
- Hero content stacks vertically.
- The decorative model is absent; the page keeps the hero focused on the offer and form.
- Proof points wrap into compact rows.
- Feature, configuration, location, and calculator grids collapse into readable single-column layouts.
- The page has no intended horizontal overflow.

## Buyer Journey Summary

The page follows this sequence:

1. Establish the project and location.
2. Show price, scale, and legal-document signals.
3. Explain why the builder is credible.
4. Describe lifestyle and infrastructure benefits.
5. Let buyers inspect plot availability.
6. Present raw-plot and villa pathways.
7. Explain connectivity.
8. Offer indicative investment calculations.
9. Convert the visitor through a site-visit form and WhatsApp contact.

## Recommended Content Cleanup

Before using the site publicly, replace all remaining Dapoli, Nargoli, Khed, Konkan, and Serene Palms references with verified Boisar East information. Reconcile the plot inventory, location graphic, calculator assumptions, footer compliance statement, and page metadata with the final Om Sai Developers sales material.
